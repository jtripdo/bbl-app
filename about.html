Buttermilk Biscuit Lab makes scratch-made buttermilk biscuits in Tampa. Find us at Pasta Packs, at pop-ups around Tampa Bay, or at your next event.

Our story

Every biscuit starts from scratch: real buttermilk, cold butter and a lot of folding.

We bake them fresh, build them into sandwiches and plates, and bring them wherever Tampa is hungry, from our counter at Pasta Packs to pop-ups around the bay to weddings, offices and game days.

The "lab" part is how we work: we keep testing, tweaking and trying new combinations until every bite is worth the trip.

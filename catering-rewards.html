The fine print

Good questions.

Who gets the credit?

The person who placed the catering order, tied to the phone number on the order. If you're new, we'll set up your free BBL Rewards account automatically.

When does it show up?

The day after your event. We'll email you the amount and when it expires.

What counts toward the $150?

Your food total after any discounts, not counting tax, tip or delivery. Orders of $150 or more earn 15% of that total.

Where can I use it?

At our self-order kiosk, on online orders (pickup at Pasta Packs or our pop-ups), and at any pop-up, where we'll look you up by phone. Use up to $20 per order, or half the order, whichever is less, so your credit keeps treating you visit after visit.

Can I use it on catering?

No. Credit is for treating yourself, so it can't be used on catering orders or online carts with catering items.

Does it expire?

Yes, 12 months after it's added. We'll send a reminder 30 days before.

Do catering orders also earn points?

Catering earns credit instead of points. Your everyday orders keep earning points: 1 per $1, double at pop-ups, and 100 points gets you a free biscuit sandwich. More about BBL Rewards.

Up to $20 of credit per order, or half the order, whichever is less. BBL credit has no cash value, isn't redeemable for cash, and doesn't cover tax or tips. Not transferable. Credits from canceled or refunded orders are removed. Terms may change; credit you've already earned keeps its terms.
